## Signs of rust

Rust diseases often create yellow, orange, reddish-brown, or dark brown spots or pustules, especially on leaf undersides. When touched, the affected area may leave colored powder on a finger or sheet of paper.

## How the disease progresses

Pustules increase, leaves yellow, and early drop may follow. Some hosts show different colors at different stages, so a “rust” color alone does not identify the species. Soil splash, spray residue, and insects can also be mistaken for rust.

## First action

Remove badly affected leaves, clear fallen foliage, and avoid overhead watering. Do not handle or move wet plants into a clean area. If colored powder returns on leaf undersides or the disease spreads, confirm the host and causal group before treatment.

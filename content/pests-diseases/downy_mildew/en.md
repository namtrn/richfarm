## Signs of downy mildew

Downy mildew often creates pale yellow or light green patches on the upper leaf surface. During prolonged humidity, a thin gray, purple, or white growth may appear beneath the affected area. The pattern varies by host plant.

## How the disease progresses

Spots may enlarge quickly, turn brown, and kill sections of the leaf. Lower leaves or dense canopies are often affected first because they dry slowly. Other leaf-spot diseases can look similar, so inspect both leaf surfaces and the moisture conditions.

## First action

Separate suspected plants, place diseased leaves in closed waste, and avoid wetting the canopy. Water at the base, improve air movement, and stay out of the foliage while it is wet. When several plants become affected after rain or long dew periods, confirm the agent before choosing a product.

The name “downy mildew” does not identify one fungus; the causal group changes with the crop.

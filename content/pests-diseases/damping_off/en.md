## Signs of damping-off

Affected seedlings may emerge unevenly, then develop a pinched, soft, or brown stem at the media line. A green seedling can topple quickly; young roots are often sparse, brown, and fragile.

## Conditions that favor it

Water-retentive media, crowded trays, deep sowing, and still air keep tender tissue wet for too long. Drought wilt usually comes with dry media and does not create a brown stem pinch. Several different agents can cause seedling collapse.

## First action

Remove collapsed seedlings and nearby media, reduce watering, and improve airflow. Use clean trays, tools, and media for the next batch; do not reuse water that has passed through a diseased tray. Give seedlings enough light and water only as much as needed for the surface to dry appropriately.

Do not use one treatment for every propagation batch. If losses are high, confirm the agent and sanitation conditions before taking further action.

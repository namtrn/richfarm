## How to recognize fungus gnats

Fungus gnats are small dark flies that hover around the surface of potting mix or rest near a container. Their larvae live in wet media and may be hard to see without checking the roots or mix.

## Signs on plants

Seedlings may grow slowly, wilt, or develop damaged roots when the mix stays wet. Many adults flying up after watering or touching the soil are a reason to inspect, although a few adults do not always mean serious plant damage.

## First checks and safe action

Place yellow sticky cards just above the mix to monitor adults. Let the surface dry as the plant allows, remove decaying leaves, and check drainage holes. For a seedling that stays wilted while the soil is not dry, inspect the roots; brown, soft roots can involve waterlogging or another cause, not fungus gnats alone.

Do not drench with a pesticide or biological product until the crop and suitable media moisture are known. Keep trays, tools, and propagation media clean between batches.

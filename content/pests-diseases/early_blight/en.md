## Signs of early blight

The disease often begins on older lower leaves as round brown spots with concentric rings, sometimes surrounded by yellowing. Spots may start near a leaf edge or vein and join into larger dead areas.

## How the disease progresses

Leaves yellow and drop from the bottom upward, thinning the canopy and exposing fruit to sun. Brown, sunken marks may occur on stems and stalks, and fruit near the stem end can develop dark lesions. Nutrient stress can also yellow older leaves but does not create the characteristic rings.

## First action

Remove diseased leaves from the growing area, mulch to reduce soil splash, and water at the base. Keep the canopy open, avoid working among wet plants, and clean shears after cutting. If spread is rapid or fruit is affected, confirm the agent before choosing a product.

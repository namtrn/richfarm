## How to recognize whiteflies

Whiteflies are small white insects that usually rest on leaf undersides. When the plant is touched, a group may fly up briefly before settling back under the leaves.

## Signs on plants

Feeding can leave leaves yellow, weak, and prone to early drop. Honeydew makes leaves sticky and can support a dark sooty coating. A single yellow leaf is not enough to diagnose whiteflies because root stress, nutrition problems, and leaf age can look similar.

## First checks and safe action

Inspect the undersides of young leaves by gently shaking a shoot and watching for flying insects. Use yellow sticky cards to monitor adults, remove leaves heavily coated with honeydew, and clear nearby weeds that may host the insects. Quarantine new plants until they have been checked.

If numbers rise quickly, confirm the insect and crop before choosing a product. Do not spray in strong wind or on harvestable tissue when the label does not allow it.

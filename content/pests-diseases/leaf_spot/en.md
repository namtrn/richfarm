## Signs of leaf spot

Leaf spot can create round or angular brown, gray, yellow, or black marks. Some spots have dark margins, dry centers, or tiny specks; the pattern depends on the crop and causal agent.

## How the disease progresses

New spots may increase in number and size when foliage stays wet. Many spots can join, making leaves yellow, dry, and drop. Insect feeding, sun injury, and nutrient stress can also discolor tissue, so inspect both leaf surfaces and the care history.

## First action

Remove clearly diseased leaves, place them in closed waste, and clean tools. Water at the base, improve air movement, and avoid handling plants while foliage is wet. If spots spread to several plants or appear on fruit, identify the agent before choosing a product.

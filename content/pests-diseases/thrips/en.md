## How to recognize thrips

Thrips are very small, narrow-bodied insects that hide in leaf folds, flower buds, and tender growth. They may run quickly or make short flights when a flower or shoot is disturbed.

## Signs on plants

Leaves may show silvery streaks, scarring, or tiny black frass spots. New leaves can become distorted, while flowers may turn brown and young fruit can develop scars. Wind, dust, and mechanical injury can also make streaks, so look for the insect before deciding.

## First checks and safe action

Tap flowers or shoots over white paper to look for moving insects, and use blue or yellow sticky cards to monitor activity. Remove badly damaged flowers and shoots, then clear weeds and residue near the crop. Recheck new growth over the next few days.

If thrips may be transmitting a virus or numbers are rising quickly, identify the crop and pest before choosing a product. Follow the label, protect pollinators, and observe the harvest interval.

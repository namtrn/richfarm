## Signs of gray mold

Gray mold often starts on spent flowers, bruised fruit, older leaves, or weakened tissue. The area becomes soft and brown, then develops a dusty gray growth; stalks and branches may also be affected when humidity stays high.

## How the disease progresses

It spreads quickly in dense canopies, standing moisture, and areas where dead tissue remains. Harvested fruit can also rot from a bruise. Bacterial decay and mechanical injury may look similar, so inspect the gray growth and storage conditions.

## First action

Remove spent flowers, rotten fruit, and diseased leaves from the growing area without brushing them against healthy plants. Water at the base, open the canopy, and handle fruit gently. When spread is broad, identify the agent before treatment, especially on tissue close to harvest.
